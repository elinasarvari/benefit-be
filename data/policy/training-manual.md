# Benefit Case Training Manual (training edition, not official policy)

Version date: 2026-01-01

This manual is a training edition written for the Benefit Case workshop. It is not official policy and must not be used for real eligibility decisions. Values match the IL adult rule sets in `packages/rules/rule_sets.py`.

## TM-2.1 Illinois residency

Training edition, not official policy. The applicant must live in Illinois on the date of the determination (rule ELIG-01).

## TM-2.2 Age requirement

Training edition, not official policy. The applicant must be 19 to 64 years old on the date of the determination (rule ELIG-02).

## TM-2.3 Citizenship attestation

Training edition, not official policy. The applicant must attest to U.S. citizenship or an eligible immigration status (rule ELIG-03).

## TM-2.4 Identity verification

Training edition, not official policy. The applicant's identity must be verified from a submitted document (rule ELIG-04).

## TM-2.5 Medicare entitlement

Training edition, not official policy. The applicant must not be entitled to Medicare (rule ELIG-05).

## TM-3.1 Household income limit

Training edition, not official policy. Household monthly income must be at or below 138% of the federal poverty guideline for the household size, divided by 12. The guideline is $15,500 for one person plus $5,200 for each additional person in 2026 ($15,000 and $5,000 in 2025) (rule ELIG-06).
